# 2. Project Title

## SURAKSHA – A Healthcare Companion for Senior Citizens Living Alone

SURAKSHA is a healthcare companion designed for senior citizens
who live alone and may face difficulties while managing their
healthcare without immediate family support.

The platform aims to connect senior citizens, their families,
healthcare providers and assisted-care services through a
single digital platform.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Senior citizens living alone may face several challenges while
managing their healthcare.

These challenges include:

- Medical records being scattered across different places
- Difficulty remembering previous medicines and medical history
- Difficulty finding appropriate hospitals
- Difficulty managing hospital visits independently
- Family members being unable to assist immediately
- Difficulty coordinating during emergencies

Existing healthcare systems may address individual parts of
healthcare, but senior citizens living alone may still require
better coordination between their family, medical information
and healthcare services.

SURAKSHA aims to bridge this gap.

---

# 4. Healthcare Use Case

SURAKSHA provides a centralized digital medical profile containing
important healthcare information such as:

- Medical history
- Previous and current medicines
- Allergies
- Blood group
- Medical reports
- Doctor and hospital history

The platform also provides healthcare-related workflows such as:

- Hospital discovery
- Appointment management
- Nurse-assisted hospital visits
- Family support
- Emergency SOS workflow

The objective is to make healthcare navigation easier for
senior citizens who may not always have a family member physically
available to assist them.

---

# 5. Key Features

## Digital Medical Profile

A centralized profile for storing important healthcare information
and medical history.

## Hospital Discovery

Users can discover hospitals based on their healthcare requirements.

## Appointment Booking

The prototype provides a workflow for managing healthcare
appointments.

## Nurse-Assisted Hospital Visits

SURAKSHA proposes a verified nurse-assisted hospital visit model
for senior citizens who require support during scheduled healthcare
visits.

The nurse can accompany the senior citizen during the hospital
visit and assist with the healthcare process.

## Family Support

Family members can stay connected with the senior citizen's
healthcare information and activities.

## Emergency SOS

The prototype demonstrates an emergency SOS workflow that can
connect the senior citizen with family and assigned support.

> The current SOS functionality is a prototype simulation and
> does not contact real emergency services.

# 13. Privacy and Security Considerations

Healthcare information is sensitive and requires appropriate
security measures.

The current project is a prototype and uses demonstration data.

A production implementation would require:

- Secure authentication
- Role-based access control
- Encryption of sensitive healthcare information
- Secure storage
- Consent-based medical information sharing
- Audit logging
- Appropriate healthcare data protection and compliance measures
